MEETING ISLAND - QUICK START
============================

What it does
------------
Meeting Island sits at the top-centre of the screen (just under the camera) and writes down what is
being said in a meeting, live, in English, Hindi and Hinglish. When the meeting ends it saves a text
file with the time of every line. It works completely offline: nothing is sent anywhere.


Every day
---------
1. Double-click "Meeting Island" on the Desktop. Wait about 30 seconds the first time each day while it
   gets ready (the small bar says so). You can leave it open all day.
2. When the meeting begins, press "Start session".
3. Need a break? Press the pause button. Press it again to carry on.
4. When the meeting is over, press "End session". A window asks for a name and a folder.
   Press "Save". The transcript is saved as a .txt file (default folder: Documents > Meeting Transcripts).

If you forget to save, or the computer shuts down, nothing is lost: next time the app offers
"Recover last session".


What it listens to
------------------
Right-click the island and choose under "Listen to":
  - PC audio (calls, videos)            : Teams, Zoom, Meet, YouTube... whatever the PC plays
  - Microphone (meeting in the room)    : a meeting where everybody is in the room
  - PC audio + my microphone            : an online call where you also speak

Choose this BEFORE pressing "Start session". Turn the PC volume up: with PC audio, a muted PC means
there is nothing to write down (the island will say "No sound heard").


Recording and the final transcript
----------------------------------
Press "Start session" and the meeting is transcribed live. By default the screen and the audio are also recorded
(right-click > Record: screen + audio / audio only / off - choose it before Start). When you press "End session" and Save, the
live transcript is saved at once. A few minutes later it is replaced by a final version that listens to the recording again,
more carefully, and tells who said each line. The recording is saved next to the transcript as a video (.mp4) if you leave
"Keep the recording" ticked. While it works the island shows a thin progress line; it waits while a new meeting is running
and when the battery is low, so it never slows your day.


Who spoke
---------
The transcript marks different voices as Speaker 1, Speaker 2... On the Save window you can type real
names next to them, and the names are written into the file.


Move it, shrink it
------------------
- Drag the island anywhere. While you hold it, it turns into a small circle.
- Drag it to the left or right edge and it becomes a slim vertical bar.
- Let it go near the top-centre and it snaps back under the camera.
- The arrow button makes it small (pill) or big (full transcript).
- Right-click for: look (Frost / Liquid glass), Hindi as Hinglish or Devanagari, edit glossary, quit.


Names and special words
-----------------------
Right-click > Edit glossary. Write one name or term per line (for example: a colleague's name,
a company, a product). The app then spells them correctly. Use  wrong=right  if it keeps hearing a
word wrongly, for example:  tecknolegy=Technology


It gets better the more you use it
----------------------------------
Open a saved transcript (.txt) and fix any mistake: a misheard name, a wrong word. Save the file as usual - that is all.
The next time you start Meeting Island it reads what you fixed and remembers it: the same mistake is corrected automatically
in every later meeting (live and in the final version). A small notice tells you how many it learned.
To see or delete what it learned: right-click the island > "What it learned from your edits".
(A common word, like "the", is only trusted after you fixed it the same way twice.)


If something does not work
--------------------------
- "No sound heard": the PC is muted, or the wrong thing is selected under "Listen to".
- No microphone: Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone > allow "Let desktop apps access
  your microphone".
- It feels slow: close other heavy programs. The first lines of a session may take a few seconds longer.
- Anything else: send the file  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MeetingIsland\island.log  to whoever supports you
  (paste that into the Windows Explorer address bar to open the folder).


Remove it
---------
Start menu > Meeting Island > Uninstall Meeting Island (or Settings > Apps > Installed apps).
Saved transcripts are never deleted.
